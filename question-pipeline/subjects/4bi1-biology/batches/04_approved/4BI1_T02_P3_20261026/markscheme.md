(a) The dissolved oxygen concentration rose from 4.2 mg per dm³ at sunrise (06:00) to a peak of 9.4 mg per dm³ at sunset (18:00). It then fell all night, back to 4.2 mg per dm³ at 06:00 the next day.

(b) In daylight the water plants photosynthesise. In the light, photosynthesis is faster than respiration, so the plants release more oxygen than they use, and the extra oxygen diffuses into the water. Oxygen is added all day, so the concentration is highest at sunset. After dark there is no photosynthesis, but the plants and the animals in the pond keep respiring, which uses up oxygen. With nothing replacing it, the concentration falls all night and is lowest just before sunrise.
