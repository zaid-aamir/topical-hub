- REJECT: respiration

## Q6

**(a)(i)** [1]

- ALLOW: hypha

**(b)** [3]
- the *Mucor* secretes digestive enzymes out of its hyphae onto the agar (1)
- ALLOW: extracellular digestion OR enzymes released onto the food
- the enzymes digest OR break down the starch (1)
- the products of digestion are absorbed by the *Mucor* (1)
- so there is no starch left around the *Mucor*, so iodine solution does not turn blue-black there (1)

**(c)** [2]
- (30 − 6) ÷ (4 − 1) OR 24 ÷ 3 (1)

**Worked solution**
(a) Fungi such as *Mucor* are made of thread-like hyphae, which contain many nuclei. The network of hyphae is called a mycelium.
(b) Fungi feed by saprotrophic nutrition. The hyphae secrete digestive enzymes onto the food outside the fungus. In this agar the food is starch, so the enzymes break the starch down into smaller, soluble products, which the hyphae then absorb. Near the *Mucor* all the starch has been digested, so when iodine solution is added there is no starch to turn blue-black and a clear circle is seen. As the enzymes spread further, the circle gets bigger.
(c) Change in diameter = 30 − 6 = 24 mm. Time taken = 4 − 1 = 3 days. Rate = 24 ÷ 3 = 8 mm per day. Check: 6 + (8 × 3) = 30 mm, which matches day 4.

- ALLOW: a micro-organism OR microbe that causes disease
- ALLOW: something (such as a virus) that causes disease

**(b)** [4]
- W: protoctist (1)
- X: bacterium (1)
- Y: virus (1)

**(c)** [3]
- fewer chloroplasts so less photosynthesis (1)
- so less carbohydrate OR glucose OR sugar OR food is made (1)
- so less is available for energy OR for making new cells OR for growth (1)

**Worked solution**
(a) A pathogen is an organism (or a virus) that causes disease. Pathogens may be fungi, bacteria, protoctists or viruses.
