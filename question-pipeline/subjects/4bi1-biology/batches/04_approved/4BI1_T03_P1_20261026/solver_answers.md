## Q18
**(a)** The pill contains oestrogen and progesterone, which keep the blood concentrations of these hormones high. High concentrations of these hormones inhibit the release of FSH from the pituitary gland. Without FSH, follicles/eggs do not mature in the ovary and ovulation (the release of an egg) does not happen. No egg is released, so there is no egg for the sperm to fertilise, so she cannot become pregnant.
**(b)** The hormones are removed from the blood (broken down or excreted) fairly quickly, so if she is late taking a pill the concentrations in the blood fall. If the concentrations fall, FSH is no longer inhibited, so an egg may mature and be released, and she could become pregnant. Taking it at the same time keeps the hormone concentrations constantly high.
**(c)** With typical use some women forget to take the pill, or take it late, or miss days, or vomiting or other medicines stop it working, so the hormone concentrations fall and ovulation may happen.
