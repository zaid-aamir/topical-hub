**(c)** [2]

- ALLOW: new plants are produced more quickly than from seed
- disadvantage: there is no genetic variation, so if a disease (OR change in the environment) affects one plant it is likely to affect them all (1)
- ALLOW: overcrowding of plants near the parent; competition with the parent
- One mark for an explained advantage and one mark for an explained disadvantage.

**Worked solution**
In sexual reproduction two parents each produce gametes. When a male and a female gamete fuse at fertilisation, genetic material from both parents is combined, so the offspring are all slightly different. In asexual reproduction there is one parent and no gametes, so the offspring are genetically identical to the parent.

Runners are a natural method of asexual reproduction. Cuttings are an artificial method: the gardener cuts a short piece of stem with a few leaves from the parent plant and puts the cut end into moist compost. Roots grow from the stem and it becomes a new plant identical to the parent.

For the farmer, the big advantage of runners is that every new plant has exactly the same genes as the sweet-fruited parent, so every new plant will also produce sweet fruit. Seeds come from sexual reproduction, so plants grown from seeds might not. The disadvantage is that, because all the plants are genetically identical, none of them has any variation that might help it survive a new disease or a change in conditions, so the whole crop could be lost.
