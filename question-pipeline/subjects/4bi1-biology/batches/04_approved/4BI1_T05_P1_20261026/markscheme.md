## Q1
- answer: C

**Rationale**
Boiling kills the yeast (its enzymes are denatured), so if no gas is produced with boiled yeast, the gas in the main experiment must come from the living yeast. Using more yeast (A) or a higher temperature (D) changes another variable but still uses living yeast, so neither can show that the gas comes from living cells. Removing the oil (B) only lets oxygen in; it does not test whether living yeast is needed.

**Worked solution**
A control is the same set-up with one factor changed so you can see what causes the result. To show that living yeast causes the gas, keep everything the same but use dead (boiled and cooled) yeast. Cooling it first means the sugar solution is not heated by the yeast.

## Q2
- answer: B

**Rationale**
Micro-organisms in a fermenter respire and release heat energy, which would raise the temperature above the optimum and denature their enzymes, so the cold water in the jacket removes this heat. The water does not enter the culture (A). Cooling does not kill micro-organisms (C); high temperatures do. The aim is to keep the micro-organisms growing quickly, not to stop them dividing (D).

Respiration releases energy, and some is lost as heat. In a large fermenter this heat builds up. The water jacket keeps the temperature close to the optimum for the enzymes of the micro-organisms, so they grow and make product as fast as possible.

Interspecific predation is predation by a different species, such as fish-eating birds, so nets over the ponds control it. Keeping young and adult fish of the same species apart (A) controls intraspecific predation. Feeding small amounts often (B) controls the quality and frequency of feeding. "Inter" means between: interspecific predation is between different species. "Intra" means within: intraspecific predation is when fish eat smaller fish of their own species. Nets keep out predators of other species.

Transgenic means genetic material has been transferred from one species to a different species, so a bacterium with a human gene is transgenic. A plant from a runner (A) and a cloned sheep (B) are genetically identical to one parent and contain no genes from another species. Crossing two wheat varieties (D) is selective breeding within one species.

## Q6
- answer: A

**Rationale**
A vector carries DNA into another cell. Viruses naturally insert their genetic material into the cells they infect, so a useful gene added to a virus is carried into the host cell. Plasmids are found in bacteria, not viruses (B). Viruses do not make restriction enzymes; scientists add restriction enzymes to cut DNA (C). Viruses are not cells and do not reproduce by cell division (D).

## Q7
- answer: D

**Rationale**
In micropropagation, the cells of the explant divide by mitosis, which produces genetically identical cells, so all the new plants are genetically identical to the parent plant. Meiosis produces genetically different cells (A). Being sterile prevents infection by micro-organisms but does not prevent mutations (B). Micropropagation is asexual and involves no pollen or fertilisation (C).
