## Notes

- The 4BI1 content has no statements worded "identify on a diagram", "label" or "draw"; structure statements are worded "describe the structure of ...", so they are marked `partial` (testable by naming and describing parts in words) rather than `essential`.
- All 14 `practical:` statements (2.9, 2.12, 2.14B, 2.17, 2.23, 2.33B, 2.39, 2.45B, 2.50, 2.58B, 3.5, 4.2, 4.4B, 5.6) are marked `partial`: they are assessed in the written papers through method, variables, results and evaluation (PDF p. 34), with apparatus described in words and results given as Markdown tables.
- 3.24 (family pedigrees) is the only `essential` point (1 of 176 = 0.6%; the table rounds to whole percentages). This judgement is flagged for human checking: a pedigree could be rewritten as a table of individuals, parents and phenotypes, but the statement is about interpreting the chart itself.
