## Q12

## Q16

**(a)** [1]
- charge (1)
- ALLOW: charged particles / electrons (in a metal)

**(b)** [2]
- the metal contains free electrons (1)
- (when there is a p.d. across the wire) the free electrons move / drift through the wire (1)
- REJECT: movement of protons or positive ions

**(c)** [1]
- the electrons repeatedly change direction / move backwards and forwards (instead of drifting in one direction only) (1)

**Worked solution**
(a) An electric current is a flow of charge. In a metal wire the charge is carried by electrons.
(b) The atoms in a metal have some electrons that are not tied to any one atom. These free electrons can move through the metal. When the cell is connected, the free electrons drift through the wire, and this flow of charge is the current. The positive ions stay in fixed positions.
(c) With a cell the current is direct current: the electrons drift in one direction only. With alternating current the direction of the current keeps reversing, so the free electrons move one way, then the other way, again and again.

**(b)** [3]
