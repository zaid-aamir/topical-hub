**Worked solution**
(a) First balance the rule on the pivot by itself, so that the rule's own weight does not turn it. Hang known weights on each side and slide them until the rule is level again; the rule is now in equilibrium. Measure the distance of each weight from the pivot. Multiply each weight by its distance to find its moment. Add the moments that turn the rule clockwise, and add those that turn it anticlockwise. The two totals are equal, so there is no resultant moment. Repeat with other weights and positions to show that this is always true when the rule balances.
(b) Left (anticlockwise): 2.0 × 0.300 = 0.60 Nm. Right (clockwise): 1.5 × 0.400 = 0.60 Nm. The moments are equal in size and opposite in direction, so the resultant moment is zero, which is what the balanced rule shows.
