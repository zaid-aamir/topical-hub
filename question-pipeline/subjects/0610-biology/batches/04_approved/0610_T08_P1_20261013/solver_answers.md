## Q18
**(a)** wilting
**(b)** On a hot, dry afternoon the plant loses water by transpiration faster than the roots can take it up. The cells lose water (by osmosis), so the vacuoles and cytoplasm shrink and the cells lose turgor (become flaccid), and the cell contents no longer push against the cell walls. The cells no longer provide support, so the leaves and stems droop. After watering, water is absorbed, the cells regain turgor and the plant becomes upright.
