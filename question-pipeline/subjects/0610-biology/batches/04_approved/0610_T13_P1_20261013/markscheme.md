## Q9
- answer: D

**Rationale**
Glucose is filtered at the glomerulus, and normally all of it is reabsorbed into the blood as the filtrate flows along the nephron. When the blood glucose concentration is very high, the filtrate contains so much glucose that the nephrons cannot reabsorb all of it, so some stays in the urine. Option A is wrong because urea is not converted into glucose. Option B is wrong because glucose is filtered at the glomerulus, and if it were not filtered it could not appear in the urine. Option C is wrong because there is no exchange of urea for glucose.
