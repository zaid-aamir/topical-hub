## Q16

**(a)** [2]

- A (always) pairs with T, so there is the same amount of each (1)

**(b)** [2]
- 100 − (27 + 27) = 46 (%), shared equally between C and G (1)
- 23 (%) (1)
- ECF: from the value of T in (a)

**Worked solution**
In DNA, every A on one strand is paired with a T on the other strand, so the percentage of T equals the percentage of A: 27%. A and T together make 27 + 27 = 54%. The rest, 100 − 54 = 46%, is C and G. Every C pairs with a G, so they are equal: 46 ÷ 2 = 23% G. Check: 27 + 27 + 23 + 23 = 100%.

**(a)** [2]

- heat in a (hot / boiling) water bath for a few minutes (1)
- ALLOW: heat to about 80 °C
- REJECT: warm in the hand / leave at room temperature

**(b)** [2]
- starch present in unripe and ripe bananas but absent in overripe bananas / starch decreases (1)
- reducing sugar increases as the banana ripens (green, then orange, then brick red) (1)

**(c)** [2]
- any two from: volume of extract tested / volume of Benedict's solution / temperature of the water bath / heating time / volume of water used to make the extract (2)

**Worked solution**
For the Benedict's test, add Benedict's solution to the extract and heat it in a hot water bath; without heating there is no colour change. As the banana ripens, the Benedict's colour changes from green to orange to brick red, so the reducing sugar content increases. The iodine test stays blue-black until the banana is overripe, when it stays orange-brown, so the starch content falls and is finally all gone. For the Benedict's colours to be compared fairly, each test must use the same volume of extract and of Benedict's solution and must be heated at the same temperature for the same time.
