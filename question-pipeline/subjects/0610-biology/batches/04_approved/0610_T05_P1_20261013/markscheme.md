**(a)** [2]

**(b)** [2]
- product is formed faster at 60 °C during the first minute(s) / more product at 60 °C at 1 and 2 minutes (1)

**Worked solution**

(b) At 60 °C, product forms faster at first (9 mg after 1 minute compared with 4 mg at 30 °C), because the molecules have more kinetic energy. After 3 minutes no more product forms at 60 °C.
