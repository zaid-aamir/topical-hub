## Q17
**(a)** Cholera is caused by a bacterium and is transmitted in contaminated water.
**(b)** The bacterium produces a toxin. The toxin causes chloride ions to be secreted into the small intestine. This lowers the water potential in the gut, so water moves into the gut by osmosis, causing diarrhoea.
**(c)** Diarrhoea causes a large loss of water from the body (dehydration), so blood volume and blood pressure fall. Ions (including chloride) are also lost from the blood, which affects the body's cells and normal functioning, and can be fatal.
