## Q13

**(a)** [2]
Any two from:

- useful substances / cell contents leak out of the cell (1)
- harmful or unwanted substances can enter the cell (1)
- the cell may die (1)

**(b)** [1]
- cytoplasm (1)

- controls the activities of the cell (1)

**(d)** [1]
- stores / contains cell sap (1)
- ALLOW keeps the cell firm / turgid / supports the cell

**Worked solution**
(a) The cell membrane controls which substances enter and leave the cell. If it is damaged, the cell loses this control: useful substances such as glucose or proteins can leak out, and harmful substances can get in. Without this control the cell may die. Any two sensible effects earn the marks.

**(d)** [1]

**Worked solution**
(a) A tissue is a group of cells with similar structures that work together to carry out a shared function, for example muscle tissue contracting.
