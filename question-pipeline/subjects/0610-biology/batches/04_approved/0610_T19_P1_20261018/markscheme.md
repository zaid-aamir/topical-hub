Consumers respire and energy is eventually transferred as heat. Animal muscle cells do not photosynthesise, and energy is not all returned to producers.

## Q12

## Q13

## Q14
- answer: A

**Rationale**
A community includes all the populations of different species. One species is a population; physical conditions alone are not a community.

## Q15

**Rationale**
