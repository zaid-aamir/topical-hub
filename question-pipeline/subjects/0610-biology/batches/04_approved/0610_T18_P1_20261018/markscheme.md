## Q8

**Rationale**

## Q9

**Rationale**
An adaptive feature is inherited and helps survival and reproduction in the relevant environment. Size alone or an acquired injury does not establish this.

**Rationale**
A thick waxy cuticle reduces evaporation. A thin cuticle and open stomata tend to increase water loss; lack of storage is not a water-conserving adaptation.

## Q12

**Rationale**
Reproductive success determines contribution to the next generation. Need does not create a useful allele and artificial selection is directed by humans.

## Q13
- answer: C

**Rationale**
Selective breeding selects parents and offspring with desirable features over generations. Gene insertion is genetic modification, a different process.

## Q14

**Rationale**
Treatment selects pre-existing or newly arising resistant variants. Resistance does not arise because bacteria choose or need it.

## Q15

**Rationale**
