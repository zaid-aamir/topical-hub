## Q1
- answer: C

**Rationale**
Members of a homologous series have the same general formula, the same functional group and similar chemical properties. A describes isomers. B describes hydrocarbons in general, which can belong to different series. D is wrong because boiling points change steadily along a series and all members have the same functional group.

**Worked solution**
A homologous series is a family of compounds such as the alkanes or the alkenes. All members fit one general formula (for example CnH2n+2), each member differs from the next by CH2, they have the same functional group, so they react in similar ways, and their physical properties such as boiling point show a gradual trend.

(a) Ethene contains only carbon and hydrogen, so it is a hydrocarbon. It has a C=C double bond, so it is unsaturated: it can take part in addition reactions in which more atoms join on to the carbon atoms.
(b) One bromine atom adds to each carbon atom, giving CH2BrCH2Br, which is 1,2-dibromoethane.
(c) Mr of C2H4 = (2 × 12) + (4 × 1) = 28. Amount of ethene = 2.8 ÷ 28 = 0.10 mol. The equation shows 1 mol of ethene reacts with 1 mol of Br2, so 0.10 mol of Br2 reacts. Mr of Br2 = 2 × 80 = 160. **Worked solution**
