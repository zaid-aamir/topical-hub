**Worked solution**
(a) Total energy = number of fissions × energy per fission = 2.5 × 10^21 × 3.2 × 10^−11 J = 8.0 × 10^10 J.
(b) Each kilogram of coal gives 3.0 × 10^7 J, so the mass needed = 8.0 × 10^10 ÷ (3.0 × 10^7) = 2667 kg, about 2.7 × 10^3 kg. A small amount of nuclear fuel releases as much energy as nearly three tonnes of coal.
(c) Each fission produces two daughter nuclei. These are radioactive, so the used fuel emits much more ionising radiation than the fresh fuel and must be handled remotely and shielded.
