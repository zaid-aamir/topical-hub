## Q2
- answer: D

**Rationale**
Winding the key stores energy in the elastic store of the stretched or compressed spring. When the toy is released, the elastic store decreases as energy is transferred mechanically to the kinetic store of the toy. A is wrong because the toy uses no fuel or battery; the chemical store involved was the person's, when winding. ## Q3

## Q6

B is wrong because the ball is momentarily at rest at the top, so its kinetic energy is zero there. C is wrong because energy is conserved; with no air resistance the total stays constant. Use conservation of energy: as the ball rises it slows down (kinetic store decreases) and gets higher (gravitational store increases). With nothing wasted, every joule lost from one store goes into the other.

## Q25

**(a)** [1]

**(b)** [6]

- nuclear advantage: no carbon dioxide (greenhouse gases) produced while generating electricity
- nuclear advantage: reliable, steady output day and night, whatever the weather
- nuclear advantage: a small mass of fuel releases a very large amount of energy OR the station needs little land

**Worked solution**
