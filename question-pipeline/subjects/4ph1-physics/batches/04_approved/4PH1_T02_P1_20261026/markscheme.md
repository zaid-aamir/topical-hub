## Q10
- answer: C

**Rationale**

Where light hits the drum (the white parts of the page), the drum conducts and its charge leaks away. Where the page is dark (the writing), no light arrives, so the drum stays charged. This leaves a pattern of charge on the drum that matches the printing. Toner powder with the opposite charge is attracted to these charged areas. Paper is then given a charge that pulls the toner off the drum onto the paper, and the paper is heated so the toner melts and sticks.

(b) Like charges repel, so toner particles with the same charge push each other apart. They spread out evenly instead of forming clumps, and they stick only to the oppositely charged areas of the drum.

**Worked solution**
(a) As Ana walks, her rubber soles rub on the nylon carpet and electrons are moved between them, so she becomes charged. Rubber is an insulator, so the charge cannot escape through her shoes to the ground, and it builds up on her body. The metal handle is a conductor connected to the earth. When she touches it, the built-up charge suddenly flows through her hand to the handle (or from it). This sudden flow of charge is an electric current, and she feels it as a shock.

(b) Charge = current × time, so current = charge ÷ time = (3.0 × 10^-7) ÷ (1.5 × 10^-6) = 0.20 A. Check: 0.20 A × 1.5 × 10^-6 s = 3.0 × 10^-7 C.
