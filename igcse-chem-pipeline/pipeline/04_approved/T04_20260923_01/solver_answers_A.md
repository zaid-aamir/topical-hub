## Q30
**(a)** The claim is accurate for the exhaust because the only chemical product released while the car is being driven is water — no carbon dioxide or other pollutant gases come from the car itself.
**(b)** The claim is not a fair description of the car's overall environmental impact, because it only accounts for emissions at the tailpipe, not the whole lifecycle of the fuel. Hydrogen does not exist freely in nature and must be manufactured — commonly by steam reforming of natural gas (which releases CO2 directly) or by electrolysis of water (which needs a large amount of electricity, often generated from fossil fuels, producing CO2 indirectly at the power station). So unless the hydrogen is produced using renewable/zero-carbon electricity, the car is not genuinely "100% pollution-free" overall, even though it has zero emissions at the point of use.
