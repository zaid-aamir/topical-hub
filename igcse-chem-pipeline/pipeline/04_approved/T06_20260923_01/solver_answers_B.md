## Q14

**(b)** Increasing pressure shifts the equilibrium to the right / toward the products (1). This is because the forward reaction produces fewer gas molecules (4 moles of gas → 2 moles of gas), so increasing pressure favours the forward reaction to reduce the total number of gas moles (1).

**(c)** Increasing temperature shifts the equilibrium to the left / toward the reactants (1). This is because the forward reaction is exothermic, so increasing temperature (adding heat) favours the reverse reaction to remove the excess heat (1).

**(d)** A catalyst does not shift the equilibrium position (1). It speeds up both forward and reverse reactions equally, allowing equilibrium to be reached faster.
