## Q26
**(a)** Elements are arranged in horizontal rows called periods and vertical columns called groups, in order of increasing proton (atomic) number. **(b)** The Periodic Table is arranged by increasing proton (atomic) number, not by relative atomic mass. Tellurium has a lower proton number (52) than iodine (53), so it is placed before iodine even though its relative atomic mass happens to be slightly larger.
