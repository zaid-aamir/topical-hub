## Q8

**(a)** [1]
- thermal (energy) (1)
- ALLOW: heat / infrared (radiation)

**(b)** [4] any four from:
- the Earth's surface absorbs energy from the Sun (and warms up) (1)
- the (warm) Earth's surface emits thermal energy (1)
- carbon dioxide / methane / greenhouse gases absorb (some of this) thermal energy (1)
- the greenhouse gases (re-)emit thermal energy in all directions / back towards the Earth (1)
- (so) less thermal energy is lost to space / thermal energy loss to space is reduced (1)
- ALLOW: heat / infrared for thermal energy throughout
- REJECT: greenhouse gases reflect the Sun's energy back to Earth / trap sunlight (do not award the absorption mark for "reflect")
- REJECT: any reference to the ozone layer / ozone hole (no credit)

**Worked solution**
(a) When the Earth's surface is warmed by the Sun, it gives out energy as thermal energy (infrared radiation).
(b) The steps are:
1. Energy from the Sun reaches the Earth. Some is reflected, but much of it is absorbed by the surface, which warms up.
2. The warm surface emits thermal energy back towards space.
3. Greenhouse gases such as carbon dioxide and methane absorb some of this thermal energy.
4. They then emit the thermal energy again in all directions, including back towards the Earth's surface.
5. This reduces the amount of thermal energy lost to space, so the Earth warms up.
More carbon dioxide and methane in the atmosphere means more thermal energy is absorbed and re-emitted, so global warming increases. A common mistake is to say the gases "reflect" heat; they absorb and re-emit it. Global warming has nothing to do with the ozone layer.

## Q13
- answer: C

**Rationale**
6CO2 + 6H2O → C6H12O6 + 6O2 has 6 C, 12 H and 18 O on each side. A has only 12 O atoms on the right (6 + 6), so it is not balanced. B is not balanced (1 C on the left, 6 on the right). D is balanced but is the reverse reaction (respiration), not photosynthesis.

**Worked solution**
In photosynthesis, carbon dioxide and water react to form glucose and oxygen, so CO2 and H2O must be on the left and C6H12O6 and O2 on the right. That rules out D, which has them the other way round. Now count atoms in C:
- Left: 6CO2 gives 6 C and 12 O; 6H2O gives 12 H and 6 O. Total: 6 C, 12 H, 18 O.
- Right: C6H12O6 gives 6 C, 12 H and 6 O; 6O2 gives 12 O. Total: 6 C, 12 H, 18 O.
Everything balances. In A, the right side has only 6 + 6 = 12 O atoms, so it is unbalanced, and in B nothing is balanced. The answer is C.

- 6CO2 + 6H2O → C6H12O6 + 6O2
- correct formulae of all reactants and products (1)
- correct balancing (1)
- The balancing mark can only be awarded if all formulae are correct.
- ALLOW: an equilibrium arrow; IGNORE state symbols

**(b)** [1]
- 24 (1)
- ECF: from their balanced equation in (a) (their number of O2 per glucose × 4)

**(c)** [3] any three from:
- fewer trees, so less photosynthesis (1)
- (so) less carbon dioxide is removed from the atmosphere (1)
- burning the trees releases carbon dioxide (1)
- carbon dioxide absorbs thermal energy (emitted by the Earth) and re-emits it (back towards the Earth) / reduces thermal energy loss to space (1)
- ALLOW: heat / infrared for thermal energy
- REJECT: reference to the ozone layer

**Worked solution**
(a) In photosynthesis, carbon dioxide and water react to form glucose and oxygen:
6CO2 + 6H2O → C6H12O6 + 6O2
Check: C 6 = 6; H 12 = 12; O 12 + 6 = 18 on the left and 6 + 12 = 18 on the right.
(b) The equation shows that 6 molecules of oxygen are made for every 1 molecule of glucose. For 4 molecules of glucose: 4 × 6 = 24 molecules of oxygen. Check: 24 ÷ 6 = 4 glucose.
(c) Cutting down and burning forests increases global warming in two ways. First, there are fewer trees photosynthesising, so less carbon dioxide is taken out of the atmosphere. Second, burning the wood (a carbon-containing fuel) releases carbon dioxide. Both effects increase the amount of carbon dioxide in the air. Carbon dioxide is a greenhouse gas: it absorbs thermal energy emitted by the Earth's surface and re-emits it, some of it back towards the Earth, so less thermal energy is lost to space and global warming increases.
