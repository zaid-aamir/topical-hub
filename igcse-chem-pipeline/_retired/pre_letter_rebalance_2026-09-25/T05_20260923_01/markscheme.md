## Q18

**(a)** [1]
- Ea = 160 − 100 = 60 kJ
- ALLOW: kJ/mol as the unit throughout this question

**(b)** [2]
- ∆H = energy of products − energy of reactants (1)
- ∆H = 20 − 100 = −80 kJ (1)

**(c)** [2]
- exothermic (1)
- because the products are at a lower energy than the reactants, so energy has been released to the surroundings (1)
- ECF from (b)

**(d)** [1]
- Ea(reverse) = 160 − 20 = 140 kJ

**Worked solution**
The activation energy is the difference between the energy of the reactants and the highest point on the pathway: 160 − 100 = 60 kJ for the forward reaction. The enthalpy change is the difference between the energy of the products and the energy of the reactants: 20 − 100 = −80 kJ. ## Q29

**(a)** [2]
- breaking bonds is endothermic (1)
- making bonds is exothermic (1)

**(b)** [3]
- energy must be absorbed to break the bonds in the reactants (ethanol and oxygen) (1)
- energy is released when the new bonds in the products (carbon dioxide and water) form (1)
- because more energy is released forming the new bonds than is absorbed breaking the old ones, the overall reaction releases energy / is exothermic (1)

**Worked solution**
Breaking any chemical bond always requires an input of energy (endothermic), while forming any new bond always releases energy (exothermic). Combustion reactions like this are exothermic overall because the amount of energy released forming the new, more stable bonds in the products is greater than the amount of energy absorbed breaking the bonds in the reactants — the surplus energy is released to the surroundings as heat.
